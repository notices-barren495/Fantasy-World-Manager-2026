## 🕹️ About

Fantasy World Manager is a single-player fantasy worldbuilding sandbox that blends simulation, management, RPG, and god-game elements. Create places, populate them with heroes, creatures, factions, cultures, quests, and events, then watch the world develop through stories, wars, and choices. This raw Windows pre-release build surfaced ahead of schedule and remains unfinished.

For players who enjoy sandbox simulation, worldbuilding, RPG systems, pixel-art management games, and watching self-created fantasy worlds evolve.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🌍 **Living World Simulation** — Build a fantasy world and observe its inhabitants, conflicts, and stories unfold.
- 🗺️ **World Editor** — Shape overworlds, interiors, dungeons, zones, and points of interest.
- ⚔️ **Heroes and Battles** — Create adventurers, send them on quests, and watch them fight for control.
- 🐉 **Creature Creation** — Design races, animals, monsters, NPCs, and other inhabitants.
- 🏛️ **Cultures and Factions** — Define cultures, religions, factions, and the forces shaping each world.
- 📜 **Quests and Events** — Populate your setting with quests, events, choices, and emergent stories.
- 🧰 **Editor Suite** — Use dedicated editors for items, weapons, armor, accessories, and entities.
- 🧪 **Unfinished Build** — Experience an early, unpolished snapshot of the larger game.
